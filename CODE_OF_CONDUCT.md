# Code of conduct

Help make this a useful, welcoming place for people learning and building with quantum technologies.

- Treat contributors respectfully, including when correcting technical claims.
- Discuss evidence and ideas without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, or threats.
- Disclose affiliations when recommending resources and avoid promotional spam.
- Protect other people's privacy. Do not publish personal information, credentials, or private infrastructure details.
- Accept corrections and respect decisions made to keep the list focused and accurate.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to enforce these standards. Raise ordinary moderation concerns with the maintainer; do not repost private information while describing a concern. For security-sensitive disclosures, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
